## 1. STRATEGIC PRINCIPLES & GOOGLE SEARCH COMPLIANCE

Drawing from `claude-seo`, `beyondseo`, and current Google Search documentation:
1. **No Speculative Ranking Guarantees:** Structured data (Schema.org) does not guarantee direct ranking boosts or rich result displays. It facilitates semantic parsing by Googlebot and LLM search agents (GEO/AEO).
2. **Deprecated & Restricted Rich Results Policy:**
   - **HowTo Rich Results:** Deprecated by Google across all desktop and mobile search results. Removed as an SEO objective.
   - **FAQ Rich Results:** Restricted by Google (August 2023 update) primarily to authoritative government and healthcare organizations. On-page FAQ accordions are retained for customer UX, conversion clarity, and AI Overview (GEO) citation, but **FAQ rich snippet display in Google SERP is not promised**.
3. **Canonical Schema.org Types:**
   - Telecom services use standard `Schema.org/Service` (with explicit `serviceType` and `provider`).
   - Softphone and CRM applications use `SoftwareApplication`.
   - Packaged telephone numbers with defined pricing use `Product` or `PriceSpecification`.
4. **Complete Link Synchronization:** Every link present in the desktop mega-menu, mobile navigation drawer, and enterprise footer maps strictly to an active, defined route in this sitemap.

## 3. CANNIBALIZATION PREVENTION RULES

To ensure search engines do not split link equity or rank the wrong page for a query, strict semantic boundaries are enforced:

### Boundary 1: `/products/ivr-system/` vs. `/products/smart-ivr/`
- `/products/ivr-system/` targets top-of-funnel broad commercial queries (`ivr service provider`, `cloud ivr cost`, `what is interactive voice response`). It functions as the comprehensive category hub.
- `/products/smart-ivr/` targets bottom-of-funnel technical queries (`multi level ivr tree builder`, `dynamic speech routing`, `business hours ivr setup`). It links up to `/products/ivr-system/` as its canonical parent.

### Boundary 2: `/products/call-center-software/` vs. `/products/auto-dialer/` vs. `/products/predictive-dialer/`
- `/products/call-center-software/` covers the entire blended inbound/outbound contact center stack (ACD, monitoring, wallboard, agent coaching).
- `/products/auto-dialer/` strictly targets SMB sales teams looking for click-to-call and progressive dialing.
- `/products/predictive-dialer/` strictly targets enterprise BPOs and high-density outbound telecallers needing statistical pacing algorithms and answer machine detection (AMD).

### Boundary 3: `/products/crm/` vs. `/products/telecalling-crm/` vs. `/products/whatsapp-crm/`
- `/products/crm/` is the umbrella platform page for pipeline and contact management.
- `/products/telecalling-crm/` targets telecaller-specific workflows (call dispositions, SIM vs. cloud calling, call recordings).
- `/products/whatsapp-crm/` focuses exclusively on WhatsApp Business API messaging templates, automated drip sequences, and chat-based lead capture.

### Internal Linking Rules
1. **Vertical Authority Flow:** Product spoke pages link back to their parent category hub and link sideways to their specific industry use-case (e.g., `/products/smart-ivr/` links to `/industry/real-estate/`).
2. **Commercial Push:** Every product and solution page includes a contextual text link and a button pointing to `/pricing/calculator/`.
3. **Comparison Interconnect:** Comparison pages (`/compare/*`) link directly to the transparent pricing calculator with pre-calculated comparative rate savings.
