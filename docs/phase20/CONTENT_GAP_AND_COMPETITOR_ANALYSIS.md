## 2. Priority Buyer Questions Not Yet Fully Addressed on Commercial Pages
1. **WhatsApp & SMS Add-On Cost Transparency (`/products/salon-software/`, `/products/crm/`):** Indian SaaS buyers frequently distrust flat subscription claims because vendors bill WhatsApp API conversations separately; clearly stating provider-based WABA conversation billing increases trust without publishing unapproved prices.
2. **Offline Resilience Proof (`/products/restaurant-pos/`, `/products/salon-software/`):** Highlight IndexedDB queue behavior visually via real product screenshots once owner supplies sanitized UI captures (Phase 15 gap).
3. **Asset Ownership & Pipeline Reporting (`/digital-marketing/seo-services/`):** Explicitly confirm clients retain ownership of their GA4, GSC, and Google Business Profile properties while connecting form/WhatsApp metrics cleanly.
