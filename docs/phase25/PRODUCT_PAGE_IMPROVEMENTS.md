## 2. Page-by-Page Enhancements Breakdown

### A. Homepage (`/`)
- **Enhanced Industry Section:** Upgraded the "Industries Served" section to prominently feature dedicated commercial landing pages for Hanva's core SaaS buyers:
  - **Salons & Spas** (`/industry/salons-and-spas/`)
  - **Restaurants & Cafes** (`/industry/restaurants-and-cafes/`)
  - **Telecalling & BPO** (`/industry/telecalling-and-bpo/`)
  alongside existing enterprise verticals (Real Estate, Healthcare, BFSI).
- **Knowledge Hub Spotlight:** Added an operational guides spotlight linking directly to the new implementation checklists (`/resources/crm-implementation-checklist/`, `/resources/salon-software-setup-checklist/`, `/resources/restaurant-pos-implementation-checklist/`, `/resources/crm-vs-spreadsheets-lead-management/`).
- **Clear Product Differentiation:** Strengthened product value propositions, pricing policy ("Contact us for pricing"), and WhatsApp demo hooks.

### B. Hanva CRM (`/products/crm/`)
- **Role-Based Workflows:** Clarified daily operations for:
  - *Telecallers:* 1-click cloud C2C dialing, call outcome logging, automated follow-up calendar alerts, and customer conversation notes.
  - *Sales Reps:* Deal pipeline stage progression, lead scoring, KYC document vault upload, and Meta WABA approved template messaging.
  - *Team Leaders / Admins:* Bulk CSV lead import with deduplication, round-robin lead allocation, DID registry, in-browser audio auditing, and geo/camera attendance.
- **Transparent Capabilities & Boundaries:** Grounded voice capabilities in verified telecom partners (CloudConnect, Ozonetel, Fonada), noted wallet-based calling billing, and TRAI DLT registration requirements. Explicitly clarified boundaries (not a generic bulk email spammer).
- **Internal Linking Integration:** Connected directly to the new CRM Implementation Checklist, CRM vs Spreadsheets Comparison, and Telecalling Industry Solution.

### C. Hanva Salon Software (`/products/salon-software/`)
- **Front-Desk & Booking Workflows:** Highlighted multi-stylist appointment calendars, walk-in waitlist tracking, and the configurable public online booking mini-site link for client self-scheduling.
- **GST Invoicing & Staff Commission Ledgers:** Detailed SAC 9997 compliant GST checkout, multi-tender split payments (Cash + UPI QR), staff portal PIN login, and transparent commission rules.
- **Back-Bar Auto-Consumption:** Explained automatic stock depletion of professional consumables (color tubes, bleach, wax tubs, facial kits) tied directly to billed services.
- **Hardware & Implementation:** Documented compatibility with standard 58mm/80mm ESC/POS thermal receipt printers and offline browser caching.
- **Internal Linking Integration:** Connected directly to the new Salon Software Setup Checklist and Salons & Spas Industry Solution.

### D. Hanva Restaurant POS (`/products/restaurant-pos/`)
- **Offline Resilience & Kitchen Routing:** Detailed the local IndexedDB offline order queue that keeps billing active during Wi-Fi drops, automated multi-station KOT thermal printing (Bar, Tandoor, Main Kitchen), and live digital Kitchen Display System (KDS) boards.
- **India GST & Dynamic UPI QR:** Documented 50/50 CGST/SGST tax calculations and dynamic NPCI UPI QR code generation on thermal customer receipts.
- **Recipe Bill of Materials (BOM):** Explained raw ingredient depletion linked to billed menu items with food cost variance controls.
- **Transparent Integration Boundaries:** Clarified that integrations with online delivery aggregators or custom platforms operate via scoped Partner REST APIs and signed webhooks, avoiding unverified native aggregator claims.
- **Internal Linking Integration:** Connected directly to the new Restaurant POS Implementation Checklist and Restaurants & Cafes Industry Solution.

### E. Digital Marketing Services (`/digital-marketing/`)
- **Measurable Deliverables:** Detailed execution scopes across Technical SEO (core web vitals, schema graph, canonical hygiene), High-Intent Google Ads (negative keyword lists, landing page alignment, conversion tracking), and Social Media Marketing.
- **Transparent Agency Standards:** Stated clearly that ethical agencies do not guarantee search rankings; focused on technical rigor and business enquiries.

### F. Software Development Services (`/software-development/`)
- **Engineering Stack & Delivery:** Emphasized modern full-stack web and mobile architectures (Next.js, React, Node.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, REST/GraphQL APIs), sprint-based delivery, and security audits.
- **Clear Positioning:** Articulated the exact distinction between ready-to-deploy Hanva SaaS products and bespoke software engineering.
