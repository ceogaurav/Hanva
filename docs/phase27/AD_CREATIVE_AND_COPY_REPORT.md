## 1. Copywriting Philosophy & Tone of Voice

For Indian SMB owners, marketing copy must avoid hyperbole, fake claims, and generic Western software buzzwords ("synergy", "paradigm shift").

Instead, the copy must follow three core rules:
1. **Concrete Operational Language:** Speak directly to daily frustrations (e.g., lost KOTs, unlogged calls, double-booked stylists, Wi-Fi drops).
2. **Indian Market Grounding:** Highlight GST compliance (SAC 9997, 50/50 CGST/SGST split), dynamic BharatQR UPI payments, and carrier C2C calling.
3. **Transparent Integrity:** Never promise "guaranteed 10x revenue", never claim "free demos" or "free trials", and universally adhere to **"Contact us for pricing"**.
