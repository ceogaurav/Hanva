## 1. Competitive Landscape in India

Indian SMB software buyers possess distinct purchasing behaviors: they are highly cost-conscious, demand immediate WhatsApp support, and place immense value on localized compliance (GST SAC 9997, 50/50 CGST/SGST, and NPCI dynamic UPI QR).

This report updates competitive positioning across Hanva’s three SaaS pillars based on verified market offerings.

## 2. Pillar 1: Hanva CRM Positioning

### Primary Indian Competitors
1. **LeadSquared:** Dominant in enterprise education and healthcare CRM.
   - *Strengths:* Deep marketing automation, enterprise scale.
   - *Vulnerabilities:* Expensive per-seat licensing (minimum seat contracts often exclude small sales teams), steep learning curve, lengthy setup process.
2. **TeleCRM:** Specialized mobile telecalling CRM for Android.
   - *Strengths:* Popular with Indian telecallers using SIM cards.
   - *Vulnerabilities:* Limited built-in KYC document management, separate costs for cloud telephony bridges, limited enterprise role portals.
3. **Zoho CRM:** Universal global CRM suite.
   - *Strengths:* Feature richness, global brand recognition.
   - *Vulnerabilities:* Generic international templates, lacks out-of-the-box Indian carrier C2C calling workflows, complex configuration.

### Common Buyer Objections & Hanva Rebuttals
- **Objection:** *"Why should I pay for CRM when my telecallers are comfortable with Google Sheets?"*
  - **Rebuttal:** Google Sheets has zero calling integration, zero call duration logging, and zero data leakage protection. When an agent leaves your company, they can take your entire customer database. Hanva CRM enforces role-based access, 1-click calling, and in-browser audio QA.
- **Objection:** *"Will my agents find this too complicated?"*
  - **Rebuttal:** Hanva CRM provides a dedicated, simplified Telecaller Portal focusing purely on the queue, call disposition, and scheduled reminders—eliminating managerial clutter.

## 3. Pillar 2: Hanva Salon Software Positioning

### Primary Indian Competitors
1. **Zenoti:** The enterprise gold standard for luxury hotel spas and national salon chains.
   - *Strengths:* Enterprise multi-unit commissary, global scale.
   - *Vulnerabilities:* Prohibitive pricing for independent salons, long implementation contracts.
2. **Fresha:** Popular international marketplace and booking tool.
   - *Strengths:* Consumer marketplace discovery.
   - *Vulnerabilities:* Charges commissions on new client bookings, lacks deep India SAC 9997 GST customization and offline counter failover.
3. **MioSalon:** Indian salon management software.
   - *Strengths:* India-centric, affordable.
   - *Vulnerabilities:* Older user interface paradigms, complex staff portal navigation.

### Common Buyer Objections & Hanva Rebuttals
- **Objection:** *"My stylists are creative artists, they won't use software on a counter computer."*
  - **Rebuttal:** Stylists don't need to touch the front desk computer. Hanva Salon Software includes a Stylist PIN Portal for shared tablets where stylists enter a 4-digit PIN, see their chair queue, and log used products in 10 seconds.
- **Objection:** *"How do I stop staff from arguing about commission at the end of the month?"*
  - **Rebuttal:** Hanva automatically calculates tiered commissions at checkout and provides daily transparent staff tallies, eliminating monthly disputes.

## 4. Pillar 3: Hanva Restaurant POS Positioning

### Primary Indian Competitors
1. **Petpooja:** The incumbent restaurant POS market leader in India.
   - *Strengths:* Huge merchant base, extensive third-party aggregator ecosystem.
   - *Vulnerabilities:* High hardware dependency, cloud reliance during broadband drops, legacy desktop codebase patches.
2. **Posist (Restroworks):** Enterprise cloud restaurant ERP.
   - *Strengths:* Large enterprise chain operations.
   - *Vulnerabilities:* Expensive minimum annual commitments, over-engineered for single-location restaurateurs and cafes.

### Common Buyer Objections & Hanva Rebuttals
- **Objection:** *"What happens when our restaurant Wi-Fi drops on a busy Saturday night?"*
  - **Rebuttal:** Unlike cloud-only tablet systems that freeze and halt billing, Hanva Restaurant POS operates an IndexedDB offline queue. Orders continue printing locally and bills continue generating; all data syncs automatically once connectivity returns.
- **Objection:** *"Food cost margins are killing us—can POS fix this?"*
  - **Rebuttal:** Hanva Restaurant POS includes Recipe BOM Costing that automatically deducts exact ingredient quantities per dish, alerting you to raw material shrinkage.
