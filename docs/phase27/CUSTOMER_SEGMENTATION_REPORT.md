## 1. Segmentation Strategy Overview

Indian business software purchasing is bifurcated between high-volume, low-touch small businesses (1–5 users) seeking immediate out-of-the-box utility, and mid-market organizations (15–100+ users) requiring custom deployment, integration with telecom carriers, and compliance with statutory data regulations.

Phase 27 establishes separate, specialized customer profiles and acquisition strategies for Hanva’s three core SaaS platforms and distinct engineering service lines.

## 2. Segment 1: Hanva CRM

### Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- **Target Industries:** Real Estate Brokerages, Financial Services (Personal Loans, Insurance, Credit Cards), Outbound BPOs & Telemarketing Agencies, EdTech Admissions, B2B Equipment Distributors.
- **Organization Scale:** 5 to 50 active telecalling agents and sales representatives.
- **Current Operating Mode:** Disconnected Google Sheets, personal WhatsApp numbers on agent smartphones, and standalone phone dialers.

### Core Operational Bottlenecks
1. **Lead Leakage & Delayed Response:** Inbound leads from Meta Ads, Google Ads, or portals sit in spreadsheets for hours before assignment, resulting in competitor capture.
2. **Zero Calling Accountability:** Sales leaders have no visibility into daily outbound call volume, connect rates, or talk time.
3. **Data Exfiltration Risk:** Telecallers have access to full customer databases on their personal phones, risking database theft upon employee departure.
4. **Disorganized Follow-ups:** Agents forget scheduled callbacks, leading to lost conversion opportunities.

### Market Positioning & Value Hook
*"Manage your leads, calling workflows and sales-team activity with Hanva CRM."*
- **Key Verified Capabilities:** Centralized webhook ingestion, 1-click Click-to-Call (C2C) carrier bridge, in-browser audio playback, KYC document collection vault, and role-based access portals (Telecaller, Closer, Manager).

## 3. Segment 2: Hanva Salon Software

### Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- **Target Industries:** Independent Hair Salons, Spas, Nail Bars, Beauty Grooming Studios, and Emerging Multi-Outlet Boutique Chains (1–5 outlets).
- **Organization Scale:** 3 to 15 stylists, therapists, and front-desk cashiers.
- **Current Operating Mode:** Handwritten paper appointment books, generic retail billing registers, and cash drawers.

### Core Operational Bottlenecks
1. **Weekend Front-Desk Chaos:** Double-booked appointments during Saturday/Sunday rush hours leading to long client wait times and negative reviews.
2. **Stylist Commission Disputes:** Complex manual monthly spreadsheet calculations causing tension between salon owners and creative staff.
3. **Back-Bar Inventory Leakage:** Inability to track consumption of high-cost hair color tubes, developer lotions, and keratin treatments.
4. **GST Non-Compliance:** Errors in calculating SAC 9997 18% GST (9% CGST + 9% SGST) and managing split tender (Cash + UPI).

### Market Positioning & Value Hook
*"Manage salon billing, appointments, staff and business operations with Hanva Salon Software."*
- **Key Verified Capabilities:** Public online booking mini-site, drag-and-drop chair calendar, stylist 4-digit PIN tablet portal, automatic consumable auto-consumption, and SAC 9997 GST invoicing with dynamic UPI BharatQR receipts.

## 4. Segment 3: Hanva Restaurant POS

### Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- **Target Industries:** Casual Dine-In Restaurants, Quick-Service Restaurants (QSRs), Cafes, Bakeries, Cloud Kitchens, and Food Trucks.
- **Organization Scale:** 1 to 4 billing terminals, 10 to 40 dining tables, kitchen prep stations.
- **Current Operating Mode:** Cloud-only tablet billing apps that freeze when Wi-Fi fails, or legacy offline desktop systems that cannot sync.

### Core Operational Bottlenecks
1. **Internet Downtime Panic:** Cloud billing crashes during weekend dinner rushes when restaurant broadband drops, halting billing and angering diners.
2. **Lost Kitchen Tickets:** Handwritten paper tickets misplaced between waiter and kitchen, delaying orders by 20–30 minutes.
3. **Food Margin Leakage:** Inability to calculate dish-level raw material costs (recipe BOM), leading to unprofitable menu items.
4. **Slow Checkout Counter:** Cashiers manually entering bill totals into external card EDC machines, causing payment bottlenecks.

### Market Positioning & Value Hook
*"Manage restaurant billing, orders, kitchen workflows and inventory with Hanva Restaurant POS."*
- **Key Verified Capabilities:** Offline-resilient IndexedDB order queue, station-routed ESC/POS thermal KOTs with duplicate-print guards, real-time Kitchen Display System (KDS), dynamic UPI QR receipts, and recipe BOM costing.
- **Scope Boundary:** Zero claims of native Swiggy/Zomato aggregator integrations unless formally verified through Partner APIs.
