## 1. Advertising Strategy & Governance Guardrails

Google Ads Search campaigns capture high-intent commercial buyers actively evaluating software and IT services in India. 

### Core Governance Guardrails:
1. **Planning Only:** No campaigns are published or activated. No advertising budget is spent without explicit written owner authorization.
2. **Data Integrity:** Where live Google Keyword Planner data or historical Ads account telemetry is unavailable, metrics are designated as estimates based on industry benchmarks.
3. **Negative Keyword Shielding:** Comprehensive negative keyword lists prevent budget waste on job seekers, student tutorials, and cracked/free software downloaders.
4. **Landing Page Alignment:** Every ad group routes strictly to its corresponding product page or service cluster, ensuring maximum Google Quality Scores.
