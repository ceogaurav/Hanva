When Google Search Console access is authorized by the site owner, execute this sequential audit:

1. **Step 1: Identify "Striking Distance" Keywords:** Filter for queries ranking in positions 11–20 with > 500 monthly impressions. Add semantic subheadings (H2/H3) and targeted FAQ schema markup to push them into top 5 positions.
2. **Step 2: CTR Optimization on Top-Impression Pages:** Identify pages with high impressions but < 2% CTR. Rewrite meta titles to include clear value propositions and Indian context (e.g., adding "GST-Ready", "Offline POS", "2026 Guide").
3. **Step 3: Eliminate Content Cannibalization:** Ensure that multiple pages are not competing for the exact same primary keyword; consolidate overlapping articles with 301 redirects where appropriate.
4. **Step 4: Image Search Optimization:** Ensure all future raster screenshots carry descriptive, non-stuffed alt attributes (e.g., `alt="Hanva Restaurant POS station-routed kitchen display screen"`).
