## 1. Executive Reporting Philosophy

Marketing reporting must provide executive leadership with actionable truth rather than vanity statistics. 

### Mandatory Reporting Principles:
1. **Zero Fabricated Metrics:** If advertising campaigns are paused or Search Console data is unavailable, figures are explicitly reported as `"₹0.00"` or `"Pending Live Launch"`. Never invent synthetic conversions.
2. **Metric Separation:** Differentiate clearly between traffic sessions, interactive engagement, WhatsApp handoffs, verified enquiries, and closed revenue.
3. **Product-Level Accountability:** Track performance across Hanva CRM, Salon Software, Restaurant POS, and Engineering Services independently.

## 3. Metric Computation Guidelines

- **Inbound Conversion Integrity Rate (%):**
  $$\text{Integrity Rate} = \left(\frac{\text{Verified WhatsApp Enquiries Arrived at Sales Desk}}{\text{Website Enquiry Handoff Events}}\right) \times 100$$
  *Target:* $\ge 65\%$. If this drops below 50%, inspect for mobile app switching issues or unclear prefilled message prompts.

- **Cost Per Qualified Lead (CPQL):**
  $$\text{CPQL} = \frac{\text{Total Paid Ad Spend}}{\text{Total Verified Qualified Leads}}$$
  *(Calculated only when paid ad spend is active; never estimated with hypothetical revenue).*
