## 3. Product Demonstration Delivery Protocol

To maintain brand credibility and customer trust, demonstrations must strictly adhere to the following standards:
1. **Zero Unverified Feature Promises:** Demonstrators must never promise native Swiggy/Zomato aggregator integrations (Restaurant POS) or multi-branch switching (Salon Software) until formally released.
2. **Grounded Sandbox Walkthroughs:** Product demonstrations must be conducted on local seeded sandboxes using realistic synthetic data. Demonstrators must never expose raw customer databases or live client records during sales calls.
3. **Transparent Pricing Review:** Standard commercial rate cards must be shared via structured PDF proposals following the demonstration call. Zero fabricated discounts or artificial urgency ("price increases at midnight").

## 4. Customer Onboarding & After-Sales Support

### A. New Customer Onboarding Checklist
- [ ] Welcome email and signed service agreement dispatch.
- [ ] Dedicated WhatsApp support group creation with customer team leader and Hanva technical support.
- [ ] Database provisioning and tenant setup.
- [ ] Initial menu/service catalog import via CSV templates.
- [ ] 60-minute staff training walkthrough (Telecaller portal / Stylist PIN portal / POS cashier terminal).

### B. Technical Support & Bug Escalation SLA
- **Critical (P0) — System Down / Billing Blocked:** Response within 15 minutes; resolution target < 2 hours.
- **High (P1) — Core Feature Degraded (KOT print delay, C2C bridge error):** Response within 1 hour; resolution < 6 hours.
- **Medium (P2) — Minor UI or Reporting Query:** Response within 4 hours; resolution < 24 hours.
- **Support Channel:** Direct WhatsApp technical desk + support email (`contact@hanva.in`).
